# Nuclear response of fixed-cusp C through the extended Fock

Each occupied-pair C matrix couples two virtual–CABS blocks of the fitted Gaussian geminal to the virtual–CABS Fock block. Its nuclear response needs both the direct geminal and the restricted extended Fock built from the moving Coulomb fit, all ordered raw Coulomb factors and the one-electron Hamiltonian. The reverse graph folds off-diagonal pair weights once, transposes the Fock, then adds its fit derivative before the Coulomb metric solve. The final coefficient weight includes the occupied, virtual and complementary legs of both contractions.

At the five-occupied neutral water root, all nine Cholesky-control C forces agree with 36 signed nuclear displacements within 3.51e-10 Eh/bohr at 0.0004 bohr and 8.20e-11 Eh/bohr at 0.0002 bohr. Independent variations of each of the two radial operators, two metric operators, core Hamiltonian and moving frame check the primitive transpose. Kinetic and nuclear-attraction derivatives enter separately. Including this source with the independently verified F, ERI, V, X and external V gives two maximum absolute mixed-force errors of 3.77e-10 and 2.53e-10 Eh/bohr. This sum still takes B and HF/CABS-singles energy derivatives from signed geometries.
