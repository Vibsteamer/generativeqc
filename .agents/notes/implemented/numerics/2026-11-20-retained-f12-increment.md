# Differentiating the explicit 3C(FIX) pair energy separately

A full unrestricted F12 calculation also evaluates conventional UMP2 and
CABS singles. A focal provider which already owns those components needs
only the fixed-cusp pair correction from the retained OBS/OptRI space.
Selecting the primal outputs before transposing sends just the requested
electronic cotangents through the shared extended Fock, fits and frame.
The complete electronic functional is the default for its existing callers.

The pair graphs use a semicanonical energy denominator. Its gauge response
inside the occupied and virtual blocks can be nonzero. The physical OBS
Fock therefore receives same-space multipliers before the remaining
occupied/virtual torque enters the coupled UHF adjoint. Small unresolved
splittings are admissible only when the corresponding torque is stationary.
The independently retained CABS kernel converts the common complementary
frame weight into an overlap derivative, in addition to the explicit
cross-spin overlap dependence of the opposite-spin pair graph.

The regression differentiates the selected F12 scalar of the original
complete program at two perturbation sizes across orbital coefficients,
one-electron matrices and both integral panels. The three selected energies
and every fixed-frame AO cotangent sum to the original electronic result.
