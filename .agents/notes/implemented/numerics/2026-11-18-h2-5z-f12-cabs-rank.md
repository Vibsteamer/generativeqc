# Keep the molecular F12 rank cutoff fixed across geometries

The H₂ cc-pV5Z-F12 qualification uses aug-cc-pwCV5Z-RI as the complementary auxiliary basis. At bond lengths of 1.4 and 1.6 bohr the complete complement contains 254 functions. A significant projected overlap direction can have an eigenvalue only about 5.4e-11 times the largest. The default 1e-9 cutoff removes some of the functions used by the untruncated independent reference.

The molecular benchmark accepts a fixed relative cutoff. A changed cutoff applies to every evaluation including displacements and response, and the output records it. Existing rank and cutoff-margin guards still apply. Qualifying a protocol requires inspecting the spectrum and matching the retained rank throughout both force stencils. A basis with unresolved rank needs a separate comparison.
