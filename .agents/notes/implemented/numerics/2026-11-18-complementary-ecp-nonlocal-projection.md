# Complementary orbital angular momentum in a scalar ECP projector

The Br and I OptRI sets include i functions. Their orbital angular momentum
is independent of the s/p/d/f channels in ECP10MDF and ECP28MDF. The quadrature
generator can explicitly request higher AO polynomials, normalization and center
derivatives while retaining the original potential channels and public staging
limits. Enlarging fixed production buffers requires its own validation.

An i harmonic on the potential center is orthogonal to all four projector
channels. To test nonzero projections, place a real i harmonic and an s function
on the opposite atom in an off-axis Br–I geometry. Compare each potential
channel, the summed matrix element, and the six nuclear derivatives with
independent integrals and two-step finite differences. This measures the
scalar pseudopotential contribution to one-electron integrals. Molecular
energies and forces also require the SCF and correlation response.

The complementary consumer uses the native regular-solid-harmonic expansion for
all 13 real i functions and its own term storage. Public orbital packers still
have their original bounds. The complete-shell regression checks ordering,
phase and normalization against libcint for d through i. Off-center tests
cover the entire i-by-s block, including all four semilocal channels, and
independently displace the potential and basis centers along each axis.
