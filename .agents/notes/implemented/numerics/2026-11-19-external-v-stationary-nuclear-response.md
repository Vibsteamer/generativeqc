# External CC-F12 V nuclear response in the orbital basis

The external V appearing in the CC residual uses the standard OBS projection. The common fixed-geminal V uses the extended-space occupied projector. Their stationary weights, integral contractions and nuclear derivatives are independent. The external transpose folds the reversed ordered-pair weight into the forward pair's transpose before differentiation. It propagates both sides of the fitted Coulomb interaction and all three radial metric matrices back to ordered AO integrals. Both orbital legs follow the RHF CPHF response.

For the fully correlated five-occupied water test, independent molecular integral sources reconstruct the entire external table within 1.94e-12 Eh. At 0.0004 and 0.0002 bohr, the analytic weighted derivative agrees with the transported signed sources within 3.16e-10 and 7.64e-11 Eh/bohr, respectively. These bounds hold for the stock and Cholesky-controlled stationary roots. In the Cholesky control the mixed force with analytic F, ERI, common V, X and external V agrees with all 36 independently reconverged geometries within 3.29e-10 Eh/bohr. B, C and the HF and CABS-singles energies still use signed derivatives in this sum. The stock control retains the earlier common-V failure at the original 2e-6 Eh/bohr gate.
