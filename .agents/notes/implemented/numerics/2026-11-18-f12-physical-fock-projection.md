# Compensated AO Fock projection for molecular F12

An orthogonal OBS/CABS frame can have large expansion coefficients over an almost linearly dependent atomic basis. For a methane triple-zeta F12 calculation the largest coefficient exceeded 1400. Independent FP64 evaluation on the archived geometry produced a scaled off-diagonal Fock antisymmetry of 3.24e-9. The CABS singles solver correctly rejects this at its existing 1e-9 gate. A 60-digit decimal evaluation of a compact two-orbital regression reproduced a symmetric result to 7.3e-18.

The molecular endpoint forms the sum of the one-electron and Coulomb operators before both orbital projections. On the GPU it carries the high and low FP64 parts of every intermediate through both contractions, using FMA to recover product roundoff. The generated occupied-space exchange remains in the same orbital representation. Physical one-electron and three-center AO inputs are checked for finite values and Hermiticity before reconciling roundoff between their triangles. The two Fock triangles are independently evaluated and the original solver admission applies to their result.

For symmetric physical AO integrals this is exactly the function expressed by the original TensorIR Fock and AO source programs. Their adjoints include both coefficient factors, the occupied trace, the fitted exchange and the density-fitting response. A two-step directional test perturbs all four inputs against the independently reassociated expression. The GPU projection adds a bounded dense Coulomb source upload and two arrays of compensated coefficients. Its allocation and time are reported separately in each central and displaced endpoint.

The same T overlap yielded a residual of 1.02e-8 in the metric of the provisional complementary frame. The complete retained branch applies one Newton orthonormalization using its physical Gram in compensated precision and forms the kernel from the corrected columns. In this branch the exact identity is `K = S^-1 - embed(A^-1)`, where A is the OBS overlap. Its reverse uses the realized K in all three terms. The truncated-rank branch retains the spectral derivative across the retained and discarded subspaces. Extended-precision independent evaluation on the T overlap yielded a maximum Gram defect of 2.8e-13 and a full-rank scaling derivative residual of 7.6e-9. The original 2e-9 physical Gram gate applies to the compensated native Gram.
