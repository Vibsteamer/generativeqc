# Bounded scalar ECP quadrature for fitted unrestricted halogen hydrides

The spherical FP64 CUDA DF-UHF prototype used ECP10MDF on Br and ECP28MDF on I with an explicit fitting basis. Both independent neutral doublets use orbital functions through f. The fitted spin kernel and scalar ECP value and gradient implementations already supply the stationary reference. A prepared calculation opts into the extended quadrature only for Br in this domain. The ordinary grid pair is evaluated first. When its elementwise convergence check fails, the last two panels are reused at 384 radial by 80 polar points, and then at 512 by 96 if required. Each adjacent grid pair must satisfy the same absolute thresholds of 2e-9 for matrix elements and 2e-8 for nuclear derivatives.

In the original BrH₂/T spherical AO model, the 160/32 to 224/44 nonlocal difference reached 1.32e-8 in the matrix and 4.09e-7 in its derivative. On the two largest grids the respective differences were 5.39e-12 and 3.99e-11. The controlled L20 full DF-UHF energy and force experiment retained both spin roots and axes. Its maximum deviation from independent PySCF analytic forces was 6.80e-8 Eh/bohr for BrH₂ and 3.86e-8 for IH₂. For both molecules the native centered differences at two spacings used 36 signed energies. These checks cover the stationary SCF term of a focal calculation.

The shape-only workspace request can select the larger CUDA bound before construction. Both ordinary panels coexist through the adjacent-grid comparison. The larger angular staging is allocated one radial layer at a time. If the last pair fails, the operator reports the quadrature rejection.
