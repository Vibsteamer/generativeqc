# Separate CABS singles and F12 pair nuclear forces

The focal energy assigns one cardinal weight to HF plus CABS singles and another to MP2 correlation plus the F12 pair increment. A joint F12 reverse pass supplies their sum. The molecular benchmark's `--component-forces` option additionally applies the singles-only pullback at the same converged DF-RHF orbitals and extended Fock. The nuclear integral values and SCF solution are reused. The isolated pullback depends on the one-electron matrix, overlap, Coulomb three-center integrals and Coulomb metric. It skips the occupied-pair kernels and the eight geminal integral derivative channels.

The DF-RHF response returns the reference force, including nuclear repulsion, plus the relaxation induced by the chosen source. A zero-source action gives the reference force at exactly that state. Subtracting it from the singles response and contracting the explicit nuclear derivatives gives the force on the CABS singles term. Subtract the reference, ordinary MP2 correlation and singles forces from the fully relaxed total to obtain the pair force. The procedure retains the conventional combined-force path and measures the additional component cost separately.

The tests differentiate the singles and pair functions independently at two steps on a small asymmetric noncanonical model. Real molecular qualification compares each raw analytic component with independently calculated signed energy displacements, preserving geometry, rank, reference, units and root. A center-only result can be checked using previously archived displaced energies.
