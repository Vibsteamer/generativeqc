# Restricted explicitly correlated CC residual components

For an ordered fixed geminal pair, the standard approximation to the unweighted two-electron matrix element uses the occupied **and** virtual ordinary orbitals in the two-electron projector. The direct matrix element contains the product of the Coulomb and correlation operators. In general that element cannot be recovered by multiplying the separately projected Coulomb and geminal matrices in the orbital basis. The Coulomb subtraction uses a raw three-center density on one electron line and its Coulomb-metric fit on the other. Contract each line with the pair matrix before summing the auxiliary index to avoid materializing four free orbital indices. The input operator, fits, raw Coulomb and geminal integrals remain differentiable. The caller must propagate the fit through its metric solve.

The extra F12b geminal residual uses ordered, spin-free spatial CC singles and connected doubles. It contains the disconnected singles product and both distinct occupied–virtual matrix elements. The latter cannot be obtained by transposing the former for a fixed off-diagonal ordered geminal pair. The projection convention for these input blocks belongs to their integral provider. Fixed singlet/triplet coefficients and spatial pair multiplicities enter when assembling the energy. The ordinary singles and doubles residual corrections use a third compiler graph. It folds the fixed singlet and triplet cusp coefficients (1/2 and 1/4) over both orderings of each spatial pair. Its off-diagonal doubles block scatters to the transposed occupied pair, and each occupied index receives a distinct singles contribution. A diagonal pair contributes once. The Fock-coupling tensor is a separate input computed from the CABS–orbital Fock block and mixed geminal integrals at the same geometry. The small restricted water source reference uses a conventional RHF Fock, direct mixed-basis Psi4 integrals, an explicitly orthogonalized cc-pVDZ-F12-OptRI space, and independently converged conventional CCSD amplitudes. The ordered-pair geminal energy graph adds the common F12a Hylleraas contribution and the extra F12b contribution to the ordinary CC correlation energy. It exposes both values separately. Fixed spin amplitudes weight the B and X rows. Contraction with the entire occupied Fock block preserves derivatives with respect to off-diagonal elements even at a canonical reference; replacing this block with orbital-energy sums in the graph would lose those derivative components. At the stationary first-order doubles limit, combining the ordinary correlation change with the F12a pair term reproduces independently evaluated Psi4 3C(FIX) pair and total corrections for He, H₂ and water. This limit checks the shared spin and functional structure. The bounded two-electron diagnostic solves the modified amplitude equations at the real H₂ geometry in the full 18-orbital cc-pVDZ-F12 space. Its independent reference acts with the two-electron Hamiltonian directly on the alpha/beta product-determinant wave function. Comparing both roots checks the residual normalization, the change from the ordinary CC amplitudes and the common/extra energy terms. The independent reference also builds the amplitude Jacobian from displaced determinant residuals and compares its Lambda solution to the generated transpose action. The input supplies independently prepared physical integrals. A general production endpoint must obtain those sources on the GPU, propagate their orbital, metric and nuclear-coordinate responses, and qualify complete electronic energies against an independent CCSD-F12b implementation.
