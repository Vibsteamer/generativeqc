# Conventional DF-CC actions in the fixed-geminal molecular response

The restricted F12 development path now has an explicit conventional CC backend that works with spatial three-center factors. The dense conventional Hamiltonian remains an independent short-molecule diagnostic. Retained blocks contain at most two virtual indices. For each amplitude iteration, a bounded batch of auxiliary factors produces the missing virtual singles and doubles contributions. The final short batch retains its own shape. Both the ordinary amplitude and factor adjoints use the same generated programs and projected spatial factor symmetry as the energy/residual. The full molecular endpoint also evaluates the geminal, SCF, CABS and nuclear terms.

For bare Coulomb three-center integrals G, write A=WG, D=J⁻¹G and WᵀW=J⁻¹. The closed-shell conventional CC contributions depend only on AᵀA. With a factor cotangent H, the bare-integral cotangent is WᵀH and the metric cotangent is −sym[(WᵀH)Dᵀ]/2. An orthogonal change of gauge in W leaves these physical sources unchanged. Rank truncation would change the mathematical problem. Its spectral response requires a separate treatment. The same formula would also require modification for a method with explicit fitting-gauge dependence.

Small random tensors test the retained-plus-batched integrals against conventional four-index CC energy, residuals, both amplitude cotangents and each physical spatial-factor sector with a short final packet. A separate full-rank metric perturbation checks the analytic Frobenius gradient at two steps and under rotation of the whitening basis. Full molecular H₂ data reproduce the previous dense correlation energy within 3e-16 hartree and all twelve physical AO or metric source weights within 2e-12. The two representations can assign different skew cotangents to the three-center Coulomb AO pair. Its physical value and every nuclear derivative are symmetric in that pair. Device time and full nuclear finite-difference acceptance are assessed separately.
