# Projected molecular ECP gradients

A CABS singles or spin-Fock source can supply its fixed weight in a rectangular orbital frame. For a frame C with public AO rows, the residual ECP contribution uses the positive derivative of W : (CᵀVC). The AO weight CWCᵀ contracts once with each local and nonlocal derivative. When the caller owns dC, each frame derivative contracts with VC(W + Wᵀ). This convention also covers nonsymmetric weights and separate alpha and beta frames. The effective-ionic Coulomb tail, changes in other Fock inputs and nuclear repulsion have their own owners.

The bent IH₂ doublet at 3.25 bohr I–H and an off-axis second H was checked in the 530-function augmented Q orbital plus OptRI space, including 39 real spherical i functions. The fixed central alpha/beta frames and other Fock sources were held while independent libcint ECP matrices were evaluated at four displaced geometries per Cartesian coordinate. Both occupied and extended virtual blocks were diagonalized again for every signed energy. At steps of 4e-6 and 2e-6 bohr the largest absolute deviations were 1.52e-7 and 3.79e-8 Eh/bohr. The Richardson deviation was 6.1e-12 Eh/bohr. The 4e-4 and 2e-4 bohr pair showed significant truncation in this fixed-frame partial. This comparison qualifies the explicit ECP component. A moving-frame regression separately checks the two coefficient terms using nonsymmetric weights.
