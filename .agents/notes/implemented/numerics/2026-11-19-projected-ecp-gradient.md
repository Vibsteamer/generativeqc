# Projected molecular ECP gradients

A CABS singles or spin-Fock source can supply its fixed weight in a rectangular orbital frame. For a frame C with public AO rows, the residual ECP contribution uses the positive derivative of W : (CᵀVC). The AO weight CWCᵀ contracts once with each local and nonlocal derivative. When the caller owns dC, each frame derivative contracts with VC(W + Wᵀ). This convention also covers nonsymmetric weights and separate alpha and beta frames. The effective-ionic Coulomb tail, changes in other Fock inputs and nuclear repulsion have their own owners.

The bent IH₂ doublet at 3.25 bohr I–H and an off-axis second H was checked in the 530-function augmented Q orbital plus OptRI space, including 39 real spherical i functions. The fixed central alpha/beta frames and other Fock sources were held while independent libcint ECP matrices were evaluated at four displaced geometries per Cartesian coordinate. Both occupied and extended virtual blocks were diagonalized again for every signed energy. At steps of 4e-6 and 2e-6 bohr the largest absolute deviations were 1.52e-7 and 3.79e-8 Eh/bohr. The Richardson deviation was 6.1e-12 Eh/bohr. The 4e-4 and 2e-4 bohr pair showed significant truncation in this fixed-frame partial. This comparison qualifies the explicit ECP component. A moving-frame regression separately checks the two coefficient terms using nonsymmetric weights.

For a relaxed molecular determinant, `ECPIntegrals.occupied_expectation_gradient` accepts the occupied and virtual coefficient matrices, AO overlap derivatives and the solved virtual-occupied coefficient rotations. It contracts the explicit integral derivative, the occupied normalization derivative fixed by the overlap metric, and the virtual-occupied relaxation. The arbitrary antisymmetric rotation within a uniformly occupied block cancels from the trace. The result is a positive energy derivative. RHF takes occupation 2 and UHF adds individually evaluated alpha and beta frames at occupation 1.

Independent signed-geometry DF-RHF and DF-UHF optimizations used the original Br ECP10MDF and I ECP28MDF triple-zeta fixtures and the archived native 224/44 ECP matrices, nuclear derivatives and orbital rotations. At 4e-4 and 2e-4 bohr, HBr Br(z) had errors of 1.19e-8 and 7.32e-9 Eh/bohr. For bent IH₂ H2(x), alpha errors were 2.48e-8 and 4.35e-8 and beta errors 3.28e-8 and 5.31e-8 Eh/bohr. With the original UHF stopping criteria the spin-resolved Richardson errors were 1.06e-5 Eh/bohr. Refining the displaced UHF occupied-virtual Fock residuals below 5e-13 Eh gave a maximum Richardson error of 5.99e-8 Eh/bohr, while retaining occupied-space and spin-root checks at each geometry. Both molecules retain per-spin translational sums below 2e-12 Eh/bohr. These comparisons cover the residual ECP expectation contribution to each determinant.

The CUDA consumer uses the same inputs with `device="cuda"`. UHF can pass a pair of occupied matrices, virtual matrices and responses in alpha/beta order, with a common AO overlap derivative. A native FP64 TensorIR program performs the AO density, metric and orbital-response contractions. Unequal spin spaces are zero-padded only along occupied/virtual axes. The initializer passes eight array blocks to the native executor, shares the AO integrals between spins and returns a single packed array containing every requested Cartesian component. The archived input matrices and orbital responses are upstream inputs to this program.

One physical L20 run of the triple-zeta occupied expectation consumer compared all six HBr and all eighteen spin-resolved IH₂ components to the CPU analytical contractions. Maximum differences were 3.89e-16 and 3.82e-16 Eh/bohr. With the executable prepared, the respective median device execution times over five evaluations were 0.623 ms and 13.392 ms, and median staged endpoint times were 1.070 ms and 14.243 ms. First calls through the public Python entry point took 5.21 s and 5.14 s including native JIT and preparation. The output transfers were 48 B and 144 B.

For a general projected weight `ECPIntegrals.contract(..., device="cuda")` also builds a single-output native tensor program. An AO weight consumes three input blocks, a fixed orbital frame consumes four, and a moving frame consumes seven. The moving case contracts both frame terms through V C (W + Wᵀ). The frozen-frame IH₂/Q CABS test uses the original molecular geometry, 530 joint functions and independent signed scalar CABS energies for all nine nuclear coordinates at both step sizes.
