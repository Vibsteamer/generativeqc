# Molecular response of the unrestricted CC fixed-geminal scalar

The scalar source in the stationary CC Lagrangian carries a single weight.
For each active occupied pair the scalar pullback differentiates the linear
V term and the signed B and occupied-Fock/X contributions. Both legs of each
robust fitted operator and the positive exchange and extended Fock responses
feed the same underlying Coulomb fit. Frozen occupied orbitals remain in the
reference and the strong-orthogonality projector. The complete active
occupied Fock matrix contributes to X.

The AO weights include both full spin orbital frames, shared AO overlap,
core Hamiltonian, bare Coulomb and four geminal three-center operators and
their fitting metrics. The scalar orbital response transports the OBS/CABS
normalization independently in each spin's CABS gauge and uses one
coupled-spin canonical DF-UHF adjoint for all nuclear coordinates.

On the distorted NH2/D all-electron doublet the central fixed-geminal
energy is −0.063703409752 Eh for the 36 active pairs. The direct AO reverse
and the relaxed response against the nine retained source directions agree
within 6e-12 Eh/bohr on the host. Independent determinant sums at the
previously solved H1-z displacements (0.0002 and 0.0001 bohr) and N-x
(0.0002 bohr) agree within 5e-10 Eh/bohr. The original vectors, separate
V/B/X controls, shell-source qualification and single-card archive
verification are in the validation area's
`fixed_source_full_cuda/source_response/molecular_cartesian_nh2/l20_fixed_scalar/`.
