# F12 focal weights apply to relaxed energy derivatives

HF plus CABS singles and ordinary MP2 correlation plus the F12 pair correction
have different cardinal dependence. A single total force at each cardinal
cannot in general reproduce the derivative of a recipe with two distinct
extrapolation powers. Store both relaxed force contributions alongside their
energies. Reuse the native cardinal weight query for each series and record
the coefficients with the ordered-surface and per-input basis identities.
Independent series may use different augmentation and upper cardinals.

The high-level minus MP2 correction uses the same high-level basis and orbital
space for its two methods. This assembly accepts that already-differenced
increment. A measured GPU or numerical accuracy claim still requires its
individual energy and analytic force to be qualified. Coordinate-dependent
weights would require additional derivative terms and a different contract.

The complementary i shells in the Br/I PP-F12 auxiliary basis exceed the
orbital range of the production ECP quadrature adapter. Generate Cartesian
Gaussian jets through i for independent operator development. The angular
switch uses a radix exceeding the largest individual Cartesian power. Runtime
spherical term staging and the nuclear/integral channels still require their
own qualification before extending an ECP molecular endpoint.
