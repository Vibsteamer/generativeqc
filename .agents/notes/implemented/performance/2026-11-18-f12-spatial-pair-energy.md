# Closed-shell fixed-geminal energy and response across occupied pairs

The ordered CCSD-F12a/b energy for spatial orbitals has fixed singlet and triplet pair amplitudes. Their action on a matrix combines its direct and transposed entries with coefficients 3/8 and 1/8. Spin contraction gives 5/8 and −1/8. Applying those two maps before materializing pair residuals gives the B contribution as 7/32 times the double direct trace plus 1/32 times the double exchange trace. The X contribution retains the entire occupied Fock matrix, including off-diagonal response. The disconnected singles product contributes to the extra F12b energy. The common geminal energy uses connected doubles alone.

The generated all-pair energy and its transpose actions serve the small-molecule restricted CC solver. The separate single-pair kernel remains useful for streamed integrals and independent algebra tests. On five occupied and 43 virtual water orbitals with 29 amplitude iterations, the solved energy required 59 graph calls, down from 124. The gathered energy amplitude VJP needs one graph before the matrix-free iterations. The relaxed source calculation takes three graph calls across conventional and geminal terms. These counts describe TensorIR graph evaluations. Device kernel counts and end-to-end device time require dedicated measurements.

An independent PySCF conventional residual and explicit spatial cusp projector recover the three energy terms to 1.2e-16 hartree on fixed water sources. All seven stationary source weights reproduce ordered pair evaluation within 7e-18. The molecular benchmark still uses dense conventional four-orbital integral blocks, which limit the admissible virtual space. Full orbital, integral, and nuclear response is validated separately from this contraction.
