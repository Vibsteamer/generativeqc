# Native DF-F12 energies and forces for single-structure training labels

Proposed on 2026-11-17. Basis combinations below are experiments. Qualification requires source-matched measurements.

## Scientific endpoint

For a geometry, charge and spin multiplicity, return the absolute Born–Oppenheimer total energy including nuclear repulsion once, and every component of its negative nuclear derivative. Each species uses its own atom-centred basis. Production inputs contain no fragments, ghost centres or counterpoise correction. Compute conformer, reaction and dissociation energies downstream as linear combinations of those same stored total energies. Atomic reference offsets used in fitting are separate data.

Target the single-reference canonical CCSD(T) basis-set limit for a declared Hamiltonian. Pin the reference determinant and root policy, correlated electrons, ECP definition and removed cores, scalar-relativistic treatment, spherical conventions, auxiliary records and numerical thresholds. Preserve the existing training convention of correlating all explicitly retained occupied orbitals unless a different model is deliberately issued. A frozen-core valence benchmark and an all-active absolute energy have different limits, even in a complete orbital basis. For all-electron centres test core-valence correlation with tight functions. A possible cheaper route is a frozen-core composite **plus** an independently converged all-active minus frozen-core increment with core-valence bases. Apply that increment only to the frozen-core base and differentiate it too. Count the ECP core once.

Use one frozen recipe per training label identity across geometries. Store component energies and forces, units, element-specific basis and ECP hashes, spin populations, reference diagnostics, precision, convergence, actual backend and qualification domain. Predictions for a larger molecule or higher spin do not inherit a small-system error bound.

## Role of the exploratory implementation

Start the production implementation from the official base. Retain the exploratory worktree so its independent reference inputs, per-operator integral checks, component energies, high-cardinal controls and known failures remain reproducible. Verify the provenance and conventions of each reused fixture. Establish new CUDA interfaces, derivative equations, orbital and auxiliary blocking, memory ownership and scheduling from the native DF and response infrastructure. The CPU prototype's host-array layout, dense work, fixed D/T weights and public API are optional design choices. Separate the independent oracle from generated GPU code so the same algebraic mistake cannot validate itself.

Prioritize two candidate schedules on the same reference cases. A uniform TZ-F12 HF/MP2/CC treatment permits cancellation of the identical MP2 contributions and sharing of reference and F12 intermediates. A split schedule retains TZ-F12 MP2 and computes the CCSD-F12 correction in DZ-F12. The former can reduce repeated work, and the latter can reduce the cost of the CC solve. A conventional triples calculation can add another CC solve to either schedule. Measure it explicitly. Include a native conventional T/Q comparator and keep the least expensive complete E/F schedule among those meeting the same raw accuracy and separability requirements.

## Component definition

For one reference and one core policy define

```text
E = [E_DF-HF + E_CABS-singles]_H
  + [e_corr(MP2-F12/3C(FIX))]_M
  + [e_corr(CCSD-F12x) - e_corr(MP2-F12/3C(FIX))]_C
  + [E_DF-CCSD(T) - E_DF-CCSD]_T .
```

Each bracket has its own orbital basis or fixed extrapolation weights. Both members of a difference use identical orbitals, basis, Hamiltonian and active spaces at that cardinal. Every correlation energy in the formula excludes HF and CABS singles. Match the perturbative limit of the CC equations to the chosen MP2-F12 definition and test any approximations to that identity explicitly. The triples term in the first production candidate is the **conventional** semi-canonical (T) increment from conventional CC amplitudes. Taking a triples result from F12-modified amplitudes defines a different comparison arm. Count the extra conventional CC solve when timing it. Benchmark a separately named alternative using the conventional triples expression on F12b amplitudes if an independent implementation can match it. When M and C use precisely the same basis, MP2-F12 definition and coefficients, their MP2-F12 contributions cancel algebraically. Reuse the F12 intermediates and measure the resulting single-basis CC-F12 endpoint without charging for a redundant stand-alone MP2 job.

Target the HF basis limit independently. Perturbative CABS singles account for occupied-orbital relaxation into an enlarged virtual space. Include the correction once in the reference energy and differentiate it. Molpro's analytical F12 gradient route specifies a separate treatment for CABS singles. For an independent full-force oracle, use energies including the correction at both displaced geometries with the same ansatz, core and fitting choices. [Molpro F12 manual](https://www.molpro.net/manual/doku.php?id=explicitly_correlated_methods)

The complementary auxiliary basis set also supplies a resolution of identity for F12 matrix elements. Turning off its singles energy leaves that distinct role in place. At 1.4 bohr the archived independent H₂ runs give CABS singles of −0.13229, −0.07463 and −0.01331 kcal/mol at parent D, T and Q. An optional separately saturated HF reference can replace HF+CABS if raw energies and gradients meet their respective budgets. Report HF-only and CABS-only values so that omission can be tested at fixed correlation treatment. [Basis convergence of the singles correction](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jctc.7b00140)

Collect single D, T and Q and both [D,T] and [T,Q] estimates for HF+CABS, MP2-F12 correlation, full CCSD-F12 correlation and the matched post-MP2 doubles increment on the tractable anchor panel. Reuse each single-cardinal calculation in both comparisons. Use a larger independent HF reference and correlate the same electrons in the correlation anchors. Stage Q-cardinal CC on the smallest cases. Profile the single T candidate even when both pairs are available. Freeze the geminal range parameter and approximation per cardinal. The exploratory CPU focal workflow weights the sum of CABS and the MP2-F12 increment with ordinary MP2 weights. Retain that calculation as a historical control.

Start with CCSD-F12b for the smaller high-level calculation and compare CCSD(F12*) on matched small cases. They have different amplitude equations. Keep native conventional (T) as its own observable. A published, basis-specific fixed multiplier on (T) is an inexpensive sensitivity arm. A molecule-dependent global MP2-F12/MP2 ratio has a size-consistency risk at dissociation and an extra derivative. An occupied-resolved correction would need its own energy, force and separability evidence.

## Qualification gates

1. **Convergence of the scientific label.** On the declared holdout domain report signed error, MAE, RMS, P95, maximum, denominator and all outliers for absolute molecular energies and separately for balanced energy differences. A proposed typical target is 0.10 kcal/mol. The requested maximum is 0.20 kcal/mol (about 0.000319 Eh) per observable under test. Use a predeclared reference uncertainty estimate from higher cardinal/independent anchors (aim at 0.03–0.05 kcal/mol). Count a case as passing only when its absolute discrepancy plus estimated reference uncertainty meets the maximum. A reference uncertainty estimate is empirical. A tiny adjacent-cardinal gap alone does not establish it. Report unresolved cases separately. Absolute errors are extensive. Report size and composition and never replace the whole-molecule test by per-atom or atom-offset errors.
2. **Forces.** Basis-convergence error in a force has its own units. As a discussion target, compare against independent, higher-level raw forces with RMS of Cartesian components at most `1e-4 Eh/bohr` and maximum absolute component at most `3e-4 Eh/bohr`. These are proposed application thresholds, independent of the energy ceiling. Separately test implementation accuracy at *identical* method and basis. Keep existing CBS node-specific tolerances, including the small reference cases with energy `1e-9 Eh`, Cartesian force `2e-6 Eh/bohr` and translation `2e-7 Eh/bohr`. State absolute tolerances and units for every new component. Re-converge independent plus/minus geometries at both `3e-4` and `1e-4` bohr along normalized directions. Check all Cartesian components on small asymmetric systems, net translation, rotation, smooth short scans and additive limits on well-separated spin-compatible fragments. Use a matched independent gradient or independent-program energy differences. A finite difference of only our implementation can reproduce the derivative of an incorrect energy.
3. **Coverage.** Use H₂ or He for exact two-electron limits, H₂O or NH₃ off equilibrium for nonzero doubles and triples, a conformer pair, a balanced reaction, hydrogen-bond and dispersion scans, a hydrated anion and a diffuse-electron case. Include open-shell OH or CH₃ doublets, a triplet with both spin channels as well as empty-spin-channel and equal-spin reductions, isolated atomic ground states with declared occupations, and a scalar-ECP species and open-shell partner on the same potential. Include production-sized neutral, charged and radical geometries to reveal runtime and memory cliffs. Partition development and holdout by molecular family. Multiple geometries of one dimer or isomer family belong to one side. Independent ground-state diagnosis and `<S²>`, occupations, root-overlap and convergence records accompany the result. Stratify or flag substantial multireference character separately.
4. **Oracles.** Use the archived independent Psi4 integrals and DF-MP2-F12 reference components and add blinded molecular and spin-resolved fixtures. Obtain Molpro or another independent implementation for the exact CCSD-F12b/F12* approximations, spin reference and triples convention under test. Use independently reconverged energies when a matching analytic gradient is unavailable. Conventional PySCF/CCSD(T) serves as an orbital-method and large-basis reference. Record source, input, method settings, basis hashes, units and output provenance. Verify redistribution rights for checked-in fixtures. Progressively compare primitives, contracted spherical shells, CABS projectors, fitting metrics, Fock and pair components, residuals, lambda/orbital response, and assembled E/F.
